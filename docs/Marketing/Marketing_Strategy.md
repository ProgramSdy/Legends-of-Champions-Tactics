# LoCT — Marketing Strategy
Version: 0.1 | 2026-11-27 | Living document

## Confirmed facts and decisions
LoCT is a Sydney-based solo/part-time dark-fantasy turn-based tactical hero combat project, moving from Pre-Alpha toward Public Alpha. It uses a single battlefield, 1v1/2v2/3v3 combat, Python engine and Next.js UI. The start page first loaded via public HTTPS on 19 November; full online readiness is not established by that milestone. PvP is a future direction, not a confirmed implemented feature. The current plan is early testing through itch.io and the existing web game.

## Positioning and audience hypotheses
Strategic Equality (Every Hero Matters), Interlocking Battle States (States Create Strategy), and Player Mastery (Mastery Beats Power) are design pillars, not proven market advantages. New heroes should expand possibilities rather than replace older heroes. The battle is won twice: in team building and in tactical execution. Target players who enjoy theorycrafting, composition, counters, timing and adaptation. Test whether these players understand and value the proposition.

| Discord | Feedback and core community | When demand exists |
| Steam | Future commercial distribution and wishlists | Prepare; verify requirements |

## Proposed execution
Run a four-week organic experiment without paid ads. Reuse short clips demonstrating actual tactical reversals, hero synergies and counters. Track channel-tagged visits, playtest starts, first battle completion and return rates; compare against time spent. Do not equate likes with demand. Obtain consent where needed and follow community rules.

## Open questions and actions
- Verify platform publishing rules, TapTap regional requirements and Steam desktop packaging options.
- Prepare representative screenshots, short clips, landing page and feedback route.
- Define target audience and test recruitment messages; avoid claiming unimplemented PvP.
- Review results before expanding channels or spending on advertising.
