# LoCT — Market Research
Version: 0.1 | 2026-11-27 | Living document

## Evidence status
No verified market size, competitor sales, audience conversion or willingness-to-pay figures have been recovered. Keep confirmed facts, sourced findings and hypotheses separate. Do not invent historical research results.

## Research questions
- Which turn-based tactical players value hero composition, status interactions and counterplay?
- Can a single-battlefield game sustain replay without large content volume?
- What are the main onboarding and retention barriers?
- Which channels attract actual players rather than only developers?
- Which audiences would pay, and for what product?

## Competitor research framework
For each title record date, source URL, genre, combat mechanics, PvE/PvP, platform, pricing, monetisation, team/content scale, update cadence, review themes, verified market indicators and relevance to LoCT. Separate observed review counts from estimated sales. Ruined King is an internal visual reference, not an established direct competitor. Slay the Spire and Into the Breach are exploratory replayability references, not validated competitors.

## Working hypotheses
Strategic discovery and mastery may drive retention; roster expansion may increase meaningful combinations without power creep. Deep status systems may be a differentiator but also a readability risk. Randomised encounters or run-based modes may improve replayability without random terrain; no genre repositioning has been approved.

## Validation plan
Recruit small external cohorts; observe first battle completion, voluntary second battles, retries, composition changes and return behaviour. Ask what was enjoyable, confusing and worth paying for. Record build, recruitment channel, cohort size and limitations. Small samples are directional, not market-wide proof.
