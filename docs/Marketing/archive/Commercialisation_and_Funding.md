# LoCT — Commercialisation and Funding
Version: 0.1 | 2026-11-27 | Living document

## Confirmed direction
Part-time solo development; government grants as acceleration capital, not survival funding. Avoid premature publisher/investor pressure or crowdfunding. August EGF round was intentionally deferred. The historical target was a round expected December 2026–February 2027; current dates and eligibility are unverified. Emerging Gamemakers Fund is a Screen Australia program, not a NSW state grant.

## Proposed commercial roadmap
Public Alpha via web/itch.io → market validation → evaluate commercial distribution and pricing → funded milestone and commercial release readiness. Steam is a candidate, not a committed release. Compare buy-to-play, expansions, cosmetics and supporter packs; no final price or model. Avoid intentional power creep and pay-for-power; even paid roster variety requires fairness assessment.

## Steam architecture implications
Current web-first Python/FastAPI + Next.js cloud architecture is useful for testing. Compare packaged cloud client, local PvE engine, and hybrid local PvE/cloud PvP. Hybrid is a proposal, not a decision. Verify Steam requirements, packaging, offline expectations, security, server capacity and operating costs before commitment. Do not assume e2-micro supports commercial load.

Prepare creative pitch deck, project plan, pitch video, CV, budget, milestones, IP/chain of title, accessibility and inclusion strategy, external playtest evidence. Confirm official templates and limits. Historical proposal: realistic six-to-nine-month milestone compatible with part-time capacity; improve readability, UI, art, audio, VFX, QA and playtesting. Demonstrate 5–8 actual hero/status combinations; avoid claiming PvP exists. Budget real hours and contractor quotes; verify eligible expenses.
